# NASA Reference Sources & Citations

The values in this game are simplified educational game approximations derived from publicly available NASA, JPL, and ESA mission telemetry and textbooks:

1. **Solar Constant & Inverse-Square Law:**
   - *Value:* 1361 W/m² at 1.0 AU.
   - *Source:* NASA Earth Observatory / SORCE (Solar Radiation and Climate Experiment).
   - *URL:* https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/features/SORCE
   - *Confidence:* Official

2. **Planetary Distances & Orbital Mechanics:**
   - *Values:* Venus 0.72 AU, Earth 1.0 AU, Mars 1.52 AU, Jupiter 5.2 AU.
   - *Source:* NASA Planetary Fact Sheet (Dr. David R. Williams, NASA GSFC).
   - *URL:* https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/
   - *Confidence:* Official

3. **Tsiolkovsky Rocket Equation & Planetary Delta-V:**
   - *Values:* LEO direct, Moon transfer ~4.0 km/s, Mars transfer ~5.5 km/s, Jupiter capture ~7.0 km/s.
   - *Source:* NASA SP-8000 Series / Space Flight Handbook (NASA Marshall Space Flight Center).
   - *URL:* https://ntrs.nasa.gov/
   - *Confidence:* Approximate (Simplified for gameplay)

4. **Deep Space Network (DSN) Telemetry & Link Losses:**
   - *Values:* Goldstone, Madrid, Canberra 70-meter dish network; one-way light delays (Moon ~1.3s, Mars ~3-22 min, Jupiter ~35-50 min).
   - *Source:* NASA JPL Deep Space Network Now / 810-005 DSN Telecommunications Link Handbook.
   - *URL:* https://eyes.nasa.gov/dsn/
   - *Confidence:* Official

5. **Radioisotope Thermoelectric Generators (RTG):**
   - *Values:* Multi-Mission RTG (MMRTG) ~110W electrical, ~45 kg mass, Plutonium-238 heat decay.
   - *Source:* NASA Radioisotope Power Systems Program (NASA Glenn / JPL).
   - *URL:* https://rps.nasa.gov/
   - *Confidence:* Official

6. **Spacecraft Flight Heritage Reference Missions:**
   - *Earth Observer:* Landsat-9 / Terra (NASA GSFC / USGS).
   - *Lunar Mapper:* Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO) (NASA GSFC).
   - *Mars Climate Orbiter:* Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter (MRO) / MAVEN (NASA JPL / GSFC).
   - *Jupiter Moons:* Juno (solar arrays at 5.2 AU) / Europa Clipper (NASA JPL / APL).
   - *URL:* https://science.nasa.gov/missions/
   - *Confidence:* Official
