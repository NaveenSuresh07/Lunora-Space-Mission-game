🚀 How to Play: Systems Engineering 101

You are the lead mission architect. Every space mission is an uncompromising exercise in engineering trade-offs:

💰

1. Budget ($M)

Every component costs money. Spend too much and your mission is cancelled before leaving the drawing board.

⚖️

2. Mass & Rockets

Every kilogram matters. The Tsiolkovsky Rocket Equation dictates that heavier dry mass requires exponentially more propellant to reach target velocity.

☀️

3. Inverse-Square Law

Solar power drops with 1/distance². At Mars (1.52 AU), solar arrays generate only ~43% of their Earth output. Heavy RTGs generate constant heat and power anywhere.

📡

4. Data Downlink

Having brilliant cameras is useless if your antenna can't beam the gigabytes back to Earth over hundreds of millions of kilometers.
